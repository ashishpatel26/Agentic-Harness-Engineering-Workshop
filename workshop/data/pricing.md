# Northstar AI pricing notes

The fictional Meridian AI enterprise pilot costs $24,000 per year plus an
estimated $6,000 per year for support. A 12-week pilot can use a smaller
evaluation license, but the price for that license is not yet confirmed.

Northstar expects a potential annual benefit of $45,000 from reduced manual
research time. These are planning assumptions, not a vendor quote or a measured
return. The ROI tool may calculate a scenario; the recommendation must label
the assumptions and avoid presenting a precise ROI as guaranteed.
