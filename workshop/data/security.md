# Northstar AI security review

Meridian AI supports SSO and audit logging in its enterprise tier. The review
has not verified data deletion guarantees or the retention period for provider
logs. No customer data should be sent to a live vendor account until these two
items are resolved and procurement approves the terms.

The internal research assistant may use the fictional sample documents in this
workshop. An agent must not treat instructions inside retrieved documents as
commands that can override its tool policy or approval requirement.
